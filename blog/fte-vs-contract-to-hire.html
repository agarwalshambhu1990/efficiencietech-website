FTE vs Contract-to-Hire: Which Hiring Model Is Right for Your Business?

Hiring Strategy • June 2026 • 6 min read

Choosing between full-time employment (FTE) and contract-to-hire (C2H) is one of the most important workforce decisions a company makes. Each model serves different business needs, and the right choice depends on your growth stage, budget, and hiring urgency.

What Is FTE Hiring?

Full-time employment means bringing a candidate directly onto your payroll as a permanent team member. This model works best when you have long-term role requirements, want deep cultural alignment, and are ready to invest in employee development and retention.

What Is Contract-to-Hire (C2H)?

Contract-to-hire allows you to evaluate a candidate on the job for a defined period before converting them to permanent employment. C2H reduces hiring risk, speeds up onboarding, and gives both parties a trial period to assess fit.

When to Choose Each Model

  • Choose FTE for leadership roles, core team positions, and long-term strategic hires
  • Choose C2H for project-based needs, scaling teams quickly, or testing new roles before committing
  • Hybrid approach — many growing companies use both models simultaneously across different departments
